General Note How to read this

Real fraud labels arrive late because chargebacks come weeks after the order, so real model objectives need delayed labels. During live operations, machine learning models and policy thresholds cannot observe instant dispute outcomes. Instead, training and threshold calibration must rely on historical order cohorts with mature observation windows to evaluate the trade-offs simulated here.
